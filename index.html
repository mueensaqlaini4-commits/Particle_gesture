✦ Gesture guide

Allow camera access, then keep your hand clearly visible in the frame.

  • Open palm: spread particles outward.
  • Make a fist: pull particles inward.
  • Move hand left/right or up/down: rotate the 3D form.
  • Use two hands: move them apart to expand, together to shrink.
  • No camera: use mouse or touch to rotate the scene.

Camera processing runs in your browser using MediaPipe. Camera access generally requires HTTPS or localhost.
